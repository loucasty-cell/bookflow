## The thesis

Make reading easy to start, easy to continue, satisfying to finish, and useful to remember.

That is deliberately not "make reading addictive". Short-form feeds win on compulsive checking.
A reading app cannot win that game, and should not try. It wins by removing the friction that
makes people abandon books they genuinely want to read.

## The four laws in one table

| Law | Principle | Bookflow mechanism | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Make it obvious | Cue visibility | Resume entry, drop zone, recent shelf | Partial |
| Make it attractive | Craving, anticipation | Chapter teasers, capsules, typography craft | Built |
| Make it easy | Friction removal | One-tap import, progressive OCR, exact resume | Built |
| Make it satisfying | Immediate reward | Deterministic progress, completion, recap, notes | Partial |

Detail and build specs: [[Atomic Habits Framework]].

## The one rule above all

Every psychological mechanism must survive this test: would the reader still feel good about it
after learning exactly how it works?

If the answer is no, it does not belong in Bookflow.
